<Info>

</Info>

The Users & Access page lets admins manage who can use Cielara and what they can do.

## User List

The main view shows all users in your organization. Each entry shows the user's name, email, role, and status.

## Adding a User

1. Click **Create**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Email Address**: the user's email.
   - **User Role**: click one of the role chips, **Admin** or **User**. See [Roles](#roles) for what each can reach.
3. Click **Add User**.

The user receives an invitation email with a registration link. They click it, set a password, and their account is active. If your organization uses SSO, the registration page sends them to your identity provider instead. See [Authentication](/authentication).

## Editing a User

1. Click a user in the list to open their profile.
2. Change their role, or set their account status:
   - **Active**: the user can sign in.
   - **Inactive**: the account is preserved but sign-in is blocked.
3. Click **Update User**.

<Warning>
  If the user you're editing is the only admin, the Admin role is locked. Add another admin before changing their role.
</Warning>

To remove someone's access permanently, delete their account from the profile view. Setting the account to **Inactive** blocks sign-in while preserving the account, and is reversible.

## Roles

Cielara has two built-in roles:

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access to all features, including Configuration pages (Users & Access, Models, Authentication, Connections setup), Impact dashboard, and admin-only API endpoints. |
| **User** | Access to Analytics, World Model, Ask Cielara, and the knowledge base, plus read-only access to Connections. Cannot manage users, configure LLM providers, or set up SSO. |
