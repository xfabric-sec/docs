## Log In to Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation email">
    When an admin creates your account, Cielara emails you an invitation link. Click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your password">
    The link opens the **Complete Signup** screen. Choose a password and confirm it. It needs at least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. Cielara confirms with **Account Activated!** and sends you to the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Go to your Cielara URL, enter your email address, then click **Next** and enter the password you just created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    The first time you reach the dashboard, a **Terms & Conditions** dialog opens over it. Read them and click **Accept**. You cannot use Cielara until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up an LLM provider">
    If no LLM provider has been configured yet, Cielara sends you to the [LLM provider setup wizard](/models#configuring-a-provider) before the dashboard becomes available. Only an admin can complete it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect a Repository

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar. This is where all your connections live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a source code connection">
    Click the **+ Connect** button, select **GitHub** (or **Azure DevOps**) from the Source Code group, and follow the OAuth prompts to authorize Cielara on your organization. Select the repositories you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Your repositories appear in the Connections list. Cielara sets up webhooks automatically to respond to pull requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See Your First Code Review

Open a pull request on a connected repository. Cielara's Foresight engine picks up the pull request automatically, reviews the diff in the context of the full codebase, and posts inline findings on the PR. You can also view the review in the **Analytics** dashboard.

## Finish Setting Up Connections

The more data sources that are connected to Cielara, the more effectively Cielara can represent your world model. Return to **Connections** and add:

- **Cloud** (AWS, GCP, Azure): discovers the accounts, regions, and resources that populate the World Model.
- **Alerts** (PagerDuty, AlertManager): brings in incidents and on-call data, and lets incidents trigger simulations.
- **Logs** (Elasticsearch, Dynatrace): adds log indices and environment metrics.
- **Workplace** (Slack, Jira, Confluence, Notion, Canva, Google Drive): adds notifications, issue linking, and the documents behind the knowledge base.

See [Connections](/connections) for the fields each provider needs.
