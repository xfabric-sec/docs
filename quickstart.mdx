## Create Your Account

Your admin creates your account from the **Users & Access** page. You receive an invitation email with a registration link. Click the link, set your password, and your account is active.

If your organization uses SSO (Google Workspace or SAML), the registration page also offers the option to link your SSO identity during setup.

## First Login

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Go to your Cielara URL and enter your email. Cielara checks which sign-in method applies to your domain. If SSO is configured, you're redirected to your identity provider. Otherwise, the password field appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    On your very first login, you're asked to change the initial password and accept the terms of service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up an LLM provider (admins only)">
    If no LLM provider has been configured yet, Cielara redirects you to the [LLM provider setup wizard](/guides/admin-tasks#configuring-an-llm-provider) before you can access the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect a Repository

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar. This is where all integrations live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a source code connection">
    Click the **+ Connect** button, select **GitHub** (or **Azure DevOps**) from the Source Code group, and follow the OAuth prompts to authorize Cielara on your organization. Select the repositories you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Your repositories appear in the Connections list. Cielara sets up webhooks automatically to respond to pull requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See Your First Code Review

Open a pull request on a connected repository. Cielara's Foresight engine picks up the pull request automatically, reviews the diff in the context of the full codebase, and posts inline findings on the PR. You can also view the review in the **Analytics** dashboard.
