title: "Introduction"
description: "Cielara is a world model for your software. It maps your infrastructure, reviews every PR, and answers questions about how your systems actually fit together."
---

Cielara connects to your repositories, cloud accounts, clusters, alerting, and docs, then joins them into a single graph. Code review, topology, and search all run against that one model.
