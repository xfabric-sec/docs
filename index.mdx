---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Cielara is a security and engineering intelligence platform. It reviews code, maps your infrastructure, simulates failures, investigates incidents, and gives your team a searchable knowledge base."
---

Cielara connects to your repositories, cloud providers, and workplace tools to give you a live picture of your infrastructure and the intelligence to keep it healthy.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line" href="/analytics">
    Your personal code quality dashboard. See antifragility scores, PR findings, severity breakdowns, and trends over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="World Model" icon="globe" href="/world-model">
    An interactive graph of your services, dependencies, and infrastructure. Click nodes, trace edges, and ask questions in plain language.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ask Cielara" icon="message-bot" href="/ask-cielara">
    An AI assistant that can query your topology, search your knowledge base, trigger simulations, and run code reviews from a chat interface.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deep Inquiry" icon="microscope" href="/deep-inquiry">
    Run structured audits against your codebase and documentation. Get reports with file-level citations in three modes: inquiry, audit, and verification.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Simulations" icon="flask" href="/simulations">
    Run scenario, foresight, and incident simulations against your live topology. Watch failures propagate through your service graph in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Incidents" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/incidents">
    Import alerts from PagerDuty and AlertManager. Cielara's RCA agent investigates root causes automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Memory Explorer" icon="brain" href="/memory-explorer">
    Explore your knowledge base as an interactive graph of sources, documents, and extracted memories.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connections" icon="plug" href="/connections">
    Connect cloud providers, source code, alerts, logs, secrets, and workplace tools from a single page.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
